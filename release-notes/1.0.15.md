## Highlights

- **Closing no longer makes the terminal jump.** "Close this window?" and every other confirm prompt used to push the terminal down by a row band while it was open, and push it back when you answered, so the shell redrew twice. The prompt now floats over the top of the terminal and the terminal stays where it is.
- **The prompt is a card.** It sits centred under the tab strip, at most 760 px wide, with rounded corners, instead of a bar across the whole window.
- **A red Close button.** When the prompt would close a terminal, a tab, a window or the app, its Close button is red with white text, so the choice that ends something stands out. Other prompts keep the theme's colours, and High Contrast keeps its own.

## Tabs

- The active tab rises into place as its highlight slides over, and sinks a pixel while you press it. With reduced motion turned on in Windows, or in High Contrast, it does not move.

## From Noctty

- Numpad keys no longer report alternate keys in the Kitty keyboard protocol.
- With a screen reader, fast typing is no longer read back to you as program output.
