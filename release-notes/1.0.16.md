## Highlights

- **A working tab spins.** When a program in a tab reports it is working without a percentage (Claude Code and other agents do), its tab shows a turning arc instead of a still ring, so you can see at a glance that it is busy. With reduced motion turned on in Windows, or in High Contrast, the ring stays still.
- **Long tab titles fade out instead of ending in "...".** The title now runs to the edge of its tab and fades into it, so a tab shows more of its name. "Administrator: C..." used to stop with space to spare; now the tab shows as much of "Administrator: Command Prompt" as fits. High Contrast keeps the dots.

## Details

- The spinner repaints only its own small icon, paints nothing while the window is minimised, and stops when the tab bar is hidden or no tab is working.
